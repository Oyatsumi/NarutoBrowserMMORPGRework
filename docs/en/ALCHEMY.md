# Alchemy 2.0

Alchemy (`alquimia.php`, villages with `towns.tem_alquimia = 1`: Mount Myoboku and Sand Village) is no longer a fixed list
of 7 fusions: recipes live in the database, with an alchemy level, success chance, refining, variant infusion, potions,
scroll copying and secret recipes. The 7 recipes from 2010 were migrated exactly (same items, 100%, free, level 1, visible).

## Tabs
- **Known recipes** — equipment, materials and scrolls; recipe books in the backpack can be *Read* here.
- **Experiment** — the 2010 free fusion (tick 1–4 items). A matching combination, even a secret one, is discovered and
  attempted with the recipe quantities. Wrong combinations cost nothing.
- **Refine** — 3 identical materials → 1 higher-tier material.
- **Infusion** — rare material + chosen weapon/vest/headband/drop → chance of a rare variant (only variants active in
  `naruto_variantes`). On failure the item is kept.
- **Potions** — potions, elixirs, Repellent Incense and Return Scroll (stackable).

## Rules
| Rule | Formula | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Chance | `chance_sucesso + 3 × (alchemy level − nivel_minimo)`, 5%–95%; 100% recipes never fail | — |
| Failure | loses `round(qty × loss%)` of each ingredient; recipes of level ≤ 5 never lose everything | `control.alquimiaperda` (50) |
| Cost | Ryou charged on every attempt; infusions add the extra cost | `control.alquimiainfusaocusto` (1500) |
| Experience | `(8 + 4 × nivel_minimo) × rate%` on success, 1/3 on failure; level up every `50 × level`, max 30 | `control.alquimiaexptaxa` (100) |
| Critical | chance of double output (not copies/infusions), at most once per day | `control.alquimiacritico` (3%) |
| Copy | blank scroll + materials → +1 of the chosen jutsu scroll (never `exclusivo_missao` jutsus) | — |

Ingredients match backpack slots by **exact name** and type, so refined (`+N`) and variant (`:effect`) items don't count.
Every action runs in a transaction with the player locked (`FOR UPDATE`) and checks the backpack *state* hash sent by the
form; consumption, output, Ryou and experience are written in one `UPDATE`. Race tests: `tests/corrida.py` (`alquimia_*`).

Database: `naruto_receitas` (see the Portuguese doc for the columns), `users.alquimia_nivel/alquimia_exp/alquimia_receitas/
alquimia_critico`, `control.alquimia*`, new drops 117–135, generated by `tools/alquimia.py`. Admin: `admin.php?do=receitas`.
